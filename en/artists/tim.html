Career History

On May 1, 2024, TiM released her first cover, “sweather weather chill” (original: “Sweather Weather” by The Neighbourhood). On December 6, 2024, the cover of “Six Feet Under” (originally by Billie Eilish) followed.

On May 28, 2024, TiM's first original song, “So Alone,” was released on all major streaming platforms.

Photo: Joyn/Claudius Pflug

TiM made her first television appearance as a singer in 2025, when she competed on the 13th season of the German singing competition show “The Voice Kids.” During the blind auditions, TiM sang the song “Julia” (originally by JEREMIAS).

On November 14, 2025, TiM's second original song, “nie wieder (Ritalin),” was released on all major streaming platforms.

Photo: Joyn/Richard Hübner

TiM appeared on television again when she competed in the 14th season of the German singing competition show “The Voice Kids” in 2026. On “The Voice Kids,” TiM made it from the blind auditions all the way to the battles this time.

Photo: zoeundmusik

TiM has already had the opportunity to perform as a support act for her "The Voice Kids" coaches, He/Ro, and for Lenny Fade.
