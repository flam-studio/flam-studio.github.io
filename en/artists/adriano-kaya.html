Career History

Photo: SAT.1

Adriano first appeared on television as a singer when he competed in the ninth season of the German singing competition show “The Voice Kids” in 2021. On “The Voice Kids,” Adriano made it from the blind auditions through the battles all the way to the sing-offs.

Adriano appeared a total of three times on the ZDF show “Die Giovanni Zarrella Show.” He made his first appearance there in September 2021, where he performed a duet with the Schlager singer on the song “Millionen Träume.” In February 2022, he took the stage with Giovanni Zarrella once again. This time, they sang the song “Die Liebe siegt” together. He was also invited to be part of the show a third time, where he sang his own version of “Alle Macht den Träumen.”

On November 4, 2022, Adriano`s first song, “Alle Macht den Träumen,” was released on all major streaming platforms. The well-known original song is from the movie “The Neverending Story.”

On March 31, 2023, Adriano`s cover album “Wie im Film” was released, featuring Adriano`s own renditions of various German film classics such as: “Kann es wirklich Liebe sein” (from Disney`s “The Lion King”), “Probiers mal mit Gemütlichkeit” (from Disney`s “The Jungle Book”), and many others.

On May 31, 2024, Adriano`s first original song, “Be My Girl,” was released on all major streaming platforms.
