Career History

Photo: Joyn/Claudius Pflug

Max made his first television appearance as a singer in 2025, when he competed on the 13th season of the German singing competition show “The Voice Kids.” On “The Voice Kids,” Bjarne advanced from the blind auditions all the way to the knockouts.

On March 6, 2026, Max released his debut album, “Home Alone (Acoustic).” On this album, he performs well-known songs such as “Beautiful Things,” “Creep,” and many more, accompanied only by his guitar.
