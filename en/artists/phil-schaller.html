Career History

Foto: André Kowalski

Phil made his first television appearance as a singer when he competed in the eighth season of the German singing competition show “The Voice Kids” in 2020. On “The Voice Kids,” Bjarne advanced from the blind auditions all the way to the knockouts.

With the Schlagerkids, which was formed in 2020, Phil released their first album together with Miguel, Lena, and Benedikta, on which they sing well-known Schlager classics. Phil, Miguel, and Lena released a second album as the Schlagerkids in November 2021, featuring winter and Christmas songs. In 2022, the Schlagerkids went their separate ways.

Foto: dennisk.photography

Since 2021, Phil has been performing at various charity and fundraising events to support a good cause.

Foto: fotowerkstattbielefeld

Am 11.11.2022 erschien Phils erster eigener Song „Einer von Tausend“ auf allen gängigen Streaming-Plattformen.

On November 14, 2025, Phil`s first solo EP, “Weck mich nicht auf,” was released on all major streaming platforms and on CD.
